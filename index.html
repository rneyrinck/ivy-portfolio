About Ivy

A recent Chicago transplant and Ohio native who turns raw footage into stories worth watching.

I'm particularly adept in post-production but can juggle all phases of a shoot — from field production and studio camera work to lighting, audio and final delivery. I'm passionate about nature, animals, the arts, and helping others, and I look for opportunities to tell stories that address social issues through creative, honest storytelling.

I love collaborating with a team and motivating the people around me to do their best work — whether that's a fast-turnaround breaking news package or a long-form documentary series.
